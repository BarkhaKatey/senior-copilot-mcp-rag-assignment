# Pump Maintenance Manual — Centrifugal Feed Pumps

## Recurring trips
Repeated trips should be investigated as a pattern rather than treated as isolated events. Review suction pressure, motor protection events, vibration, bearing temperature and mechanical alignment.

## Suction system inspection
Inspect the suction strainer for blockage and confirm inlet valves are correctly positioned. Compare instrument readings with a local reference when available.

## Motor inspection
Check relay trip history, winding temperature, current, coupling and mechanical load. Do not bypass protective trips.

## Evidence and escalation
Record alarm IDs, timestamps, operating state and corrective actions. Escalate repeated high-severity alarms to reliability engineering.
