# Boiler Feed Pump 101 Operating Procedure

## Scope
This procedure applies to Boiler Feed Pump 101 in EastRefinery Unit 2.

## Low suction pressure
When low suction pressure persists, verify upstream feed conditions, suction pressure, inlet valve position, and suction strainer condition. Do not repeatedly reset the pump until the minimum suction pressure condition is restored.

## Motor trip
For a motor trip, verify the motor protection relay, electrical supply, motor load, and coupling condition. A restart should follow the approved reset and restart sequence after the initiating condition is understood.

## High vibration or bearing temperature
Check lubrication, cooling flow, coupling alignment, bearing condition, and the corresponding instrumentation before continued operation.

## Safety
Operators must follow site isolation, permit-to-work, and approved restart requirements. This document is guidance and does not replace site safety rules.
