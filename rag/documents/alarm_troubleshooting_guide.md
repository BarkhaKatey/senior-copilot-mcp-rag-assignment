# Alarm Troubleshooting Guide

## Recurring high-severity alarms
Use the alarm history and correlation results to identify whether multiple alarms occur in the same operating window. Correlated alarms can indicate a common initiating condition.

## Pressure alarms
For pressure deviation, validate transmitter health, upstream/downstream valve state and process restrictions before changing operating limits.

## Motor trips
Motor trips require checking electrical protection, process load and mechanical condition. Follow the approved restart procedure after the initiating cause is assessed.
